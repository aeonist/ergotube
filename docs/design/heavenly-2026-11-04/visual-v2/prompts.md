# ErgoTube — visual sketches v2

Built-in image_gen tool. Three static art-direction studies; no application implementation. The user's screenshot is the layout/content reference and an anti-reference for flat surfaces. The model renders the texture in these studies; it is not the physical Tri-X scan used in v1.

## Засвеченная плёнка

```text
Use case: ui-mockup, high-fidelity static art-direction study for ErgoTube.
Input image is the geometric layout reference and content source. Its flat bland surface treatment is specifically rejected. Produce a new visually convincing material treatment on EXACTLY the same two side-by-side portrait feed screens, light on left and dark on right, straight-on with no perspective. Keep the compact Russian heading "Главная", the ocean-sunset and golden brutalist-building thumbnails in their existing positions, the existing title/avatar rows, bottom category chips and bottom dock with exactly the same five icons and Russian labels. No extra UI, no rearrangement, no devices, no ornaments, no marketing text. Preserve sharp original photographic thumbnails without overlay grain, color grading or optical distortion. Only the interface behind and around them changes.
CRITICAL: This is for visually judging MATERIAL AND LIGHT, not testing compatibility or functionality. The previous reference looks like solid beige and solid black. The new design must visibly, unmistakably show its material and warm frame-derived ambient illumination at first glance, even at small size. The shell behind the titles is continuous, with no opaque rectangular metadata strips hiding the light. Let the sunset frame's amber light and the sea's muted green-grey shadows visibly illuminate the screen margins, header and background behind text. Legible dark text in light theme and light text in dark theme. Same natural thumbnails in both themes.
Render a premium, restrained, physically convincing, richly detailed image. Do not replace subtlety with weak invisible effects. No arbitrary purple-blue gradients, neon, sparkle icons, generic decorative orbs, plastic buttons, all-over glow, dust or scratches. Preserve the flat front-facing app composition; the material itself may have depth. Keep thin screen outlines and a quiet warm-neutral outside board.
DIRECTION: EXPOSED FILM / SUNLIT ATMOSPHERE. The screen is luminous air caught on a fine 35mm film emulsion, not glass. Pronounced but elegant amber/copper light bleed expands from the sunset image into the surrounding interface; light side cream, pale warm peach and grey-green; dark side deep olive-black, visible warm amber illumination with cool muted olive shadows. Fine organic monochrome film texture ONLY on background, clean media and type. Gently asymmetrical, broad photographic light fields: light must be visible behind the header and between cards, with nuanced density like an Anthropic model launch film. No literal horizon or decorative image behind the feed. Existing "Главная" in an elegant readable serif, titles in humanist sans. A light, translucent smoky/milky navigation sheet, without shiny lens edges, seems separated from the coloured air by a broad soft offset shadow and delicate reflected warmth. Selected Home uses a small copper mark. Show an unmistakable richly lit film-world, not a slightly tinted neutral flat theme. Landscape comparison board, approximately square proportions like the reference.
```
