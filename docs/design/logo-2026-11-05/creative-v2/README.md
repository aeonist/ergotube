# ErgoTube — three more creative logo directions

Created on 5 November 2026 with the built-in image generation tool after the user requested more creative alternatives to the first e + play concept.

## Directions

1. **Chameleon** — a relaxed, curious mascot whose ability to change color connects to the application's video-derived ambient color. Tangerine, cream and midnight olive. [Presentation](chameleon.png).
2. **Personal cinema chair** — a cushioned armchair with a play-shaped seat aperture. Comfort becomes the primary symbol, connecting to the Ergo name. Burgundy and warm peach. [Presentation](cinema-chair.png).
3. **ET monogram** — a compact interlocking ligature with forward motion and vintage cinema graphics as its reference. Cobalt and chartreuse. [Presentation](et-monogram.png).

The cinema chair is the recommended direction for further development: it ties the video and ergonomic ideas together in a distinctive soft silhouette. The chameleon offers the strongest character-led alternative.

## Scope and validation

All three are exploratory raster presentations, pending the user's selection. Visually reviewed the three outputs for distinct concepts, correct ErgoTube wordmarks and coherent monochrome samples. Production vector geometry, launcher masking and small-size optical correction remain part of a future implementation of the selected direction. Android source and resources are outside this proposal, so application builds, tests and emulator checks do not apply.
