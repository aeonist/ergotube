## 1. cloud-play

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a distinctive original logo concept for "ErgoTube", a beautifully made personal Android video player. The user likes a cozy cinema-sofa symbol, warm colors and a soft vintage serif wordmark. They now ask for a new logo built around A CLOUD. Brand mood: a personal cinema floating on a cloud; comfort, lightness, warmth and effortless watching.
Design a professional compact emblem with a memorable silhouette that can become an app icon. Bold simple positive and negative shapes, beautiful optically corrected curves, soft without being childish. Flat vector-like graphic treatment only. A cloud is the identity, not a painted sky or an app screenshot.
Square presentation: one large centered emblem in the upper-middle with generous breathing room, a smaller beautiful wordmark "ErgoTube" underneath, and a tiny identical single-color emblem in the lower-right as an icon-size sample. Same warm, rounded, weighty vintage serif typographic character as an independent cinema: generous lowercase bowls, gently expressive serifs, readable normal case. Text EXACTLY "ErgoTube". No other text.
Avoid stock cloud-storage logos, upload/download arrows, Wi-Fi signals, dotted outlines, circular app badges, generic cloud plus a triangle pasted on top, Google-like multicolor lobes, suns/rain/weather symbols, wings, literal hands, faces or eyes. No mockup, no phone, no scenery, no gradients, no shadows, no glass, no glow, no 3D, no decorative texture, no watermark, no labels. Crisp flat logo shapes.
DIRECTION: THE SOFT CLOUD / play carved from air. A single chunky asymmetric cloud with three softly inflated top lobes and a slightly upswept bottom edge. Its negative space forms an elegant right-facing play aperture, integrated into the cloud's internal flow rather than looking like a separate triangle pasted over a stock cloud. The play counter has gently rounded corners and one side subtly follows the cloud's lower sweeping curve. Design the cloud's silhouette from scratch: a broad plush large lobe offset to the left, a lower small lobe on the right; compact, almost square, with a beautiful sculptural balance. Very few shapes and no outlines. It should immediately read as an airy, friendly cloud and only then a clever video sign.
PALETTE: warm burnt terracotta #B85037 emblem and wordmark on pale vanilla #F4E4C6. Tiny sample also terracotta. Flat two-color print.
